Privacy Policy

Home Inventory: Box Labels · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Home Inventory: Box Labels ("the app") is made by Komol Ibragimov. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we don't collect anything.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. We don't run a server, so there is nowhere for your data to be sent to us.

Where your data is stored

  • The rooms, boxes, items, photos and receipts you add are stored on your device.
  • If iCloud is turned on for the app, Apple syncs them through your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is protected by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't see or access it.
  • Files you export (PDF report, CSV spreadsheet, box labels) are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera to photograph your belongings and to scan the QR labels on your boxes, and the photo picker to add pictures you select. Photos are resized and stored with your inventory as described above. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Full Version" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether the purchase is owned.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete items, boxes or rooms in the app, or delete the app. To remove the synced copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone or iPad and delete the app's data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. We will never start collecting data without updating this policy and the app's App Store privacy label first.
